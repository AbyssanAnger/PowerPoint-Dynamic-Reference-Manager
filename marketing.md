## 1. LinkedIn Post

**Headline: Stop wasting time manually numbering citations in PowerPoint! 🛑**

As engineers and researchers, we love BibTeX for LaTeX, but PowerPoint makes citation management a nightmare. Manually updating `[1]`, `[2]`, `[3]` every time you add a slide? No thanks.

I decided to fix this.

🚀 Introducing **PowerPoint Dynamic Reference Manager** – a free, open-source Add-In I built to bring the power of BibTeX to PowerPoint.

**Key Features:**

- ✅ **BibTeX Integration**: Load your `.bib` file directly (Zotero/Citavi export).
- ✅ **Dynamic Updates**: Add citations like `[Smith2020]`. One click, and they become `[1]`.
- ✅ **Auto-Bibliography**: Generates a perfectly formatted references slide at the end.
- ✅ **Web Support**: Now supports URLs for online sources!

It's completely **Open Source**. I'd love your feedback!
