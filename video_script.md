# Video Script: PowerPoint Dynamic Reference Manager

**Goal:** Authentic, professional, and concise.

## 0:00 - The Problem (Authentic Intro)
**Visual:**  
Camera on you (or screen recording) showing you deleting numbers and re-typing them in PowerPoint.  
**Audio:**  
"I honestly built this tool because I was tired of manually re-numbering citations every time I added a new slide. It drove me crazy."

## 0:15 - The Solution
**Visual:**  
Switch to screen recording. You click the "Generate" button, and everything instantly updates.  
**Audio:**  
"So I wrote a VBA macro to do it for me. It’s called the Dynamic Reference Manager. It reads your BibTeX file and handles the numbering automatically."

## 0:30 - How to Install
**Visual:**  
Fast-forward (Speed x2) of adding the `.ppam` file in `Options > Add-ins`.  
**Audio:**  
"It's super easy to install. Just download the Add-In file, add it to PowerPoint, and you get this new tab right here."

## 0:45 - Workflow Demo
**Visual:**  
1. Click "Select Bib File".
2. Type `[Key]` in a text box.
3. Click "Update".
4. Show Bibliography slide.  
**Audio:**  
"Workflow is simple: Load your `.bib` file, use your citation keys like `[Smith2020]`, and click Update. It replaces the text with numbers and generates the bibliography at the end. I have decided on using "[Key]" as the placeholder for the citation keys, because it the least amount of clutter and is easy to read."
